Can we compare bone outcomes with exercise vs no exercise?

Not yet scientifically identifiable. We can compare exercise schedules (MPC versus 0 min/day), but we cannot generate two credible BMD, quality or biomarker outcome trajectories from these sources. The published Axpe curve is not a no-exercise control; the TBone postflight recovery curve is not a treatment-response model.

These panels deliberately do not invent an exercise benefit. A future intervention model requires controlled/linked data with exercise dose, anatomical site, observation time, outcomes and uncertainty.
